# 🏠 Rent Calculator (Python)

A simple Python project designed to solve real-life expense sharing problems in shared living spaces like hostels, PGs, or rented apartments.

---

## ❗ Problem

In shared living situations, managing monthly expenses becomes difficult due to:

- Confusion in splitting rent and utility bills  
- No proper tracking of total household expenses  
- Manual calculations leading to errors  
- Lack of transparency among roommates  
- Difficulty in ensuring fair cost sharing  

As a result, dividing expenses like rent, electricity, water, groceries, and food becomes complicated and time-consuming.

---

## 💡 Solution

This **Rent Calculator** solves these issues by:

- Automatically calculating total monthly expenses  
- Combining all household cost categories in one system  
- Splitting total expenses equally among all members  
- Providing accurate per-person cost instantly  
- Reducing manual errors and confusion  

It makes expense sharing **simple, fair, and transparent**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 Rent calculation  
- 🍽️ Food expense tracking  
- ⚡ Electricity bill calculation  
- 💧 Water bill calculation (units × rate)  
- 🥦 Vegetables and groceries tracking  
- 👥 Equal splitting among members  
- 🧠 Simple and beginner-friendly Python logic  

---

## 🧠 How It Works

- User enters all monthly expenses  
- Water bill is calculated separately using units × rate  
- All expenses are added together  
- Total is divided by number of people  
- Final per-person expense is displayed
